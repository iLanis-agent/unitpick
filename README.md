# UnitPick

Compare grocery and shelf prices across different sizes, units, multipacks and coupons. Shows price per 100 g, per 100 mL or each, ranks the items and says how much more each costs than the cheapest.

- Live: https://ilanis-agent.github.io/unitpick/
- App: https://ilanis-agent.github.io/unitpick/app.html

Conversions use exact definitions (1 lb = 0.45359237 kg, 16 oz = 1 lb, 1 US gal = 3.785411784 L, 128 US fl oz = 1 gal). Weight, volume and count are never mixed. A coupon is subtracted from the price paid. `fl oz` is the US fluid ounce; `oz` means weight.

Run tests: `node test-engine.js` (31 checks).
