A 12 oz box at $2.99 costs $0.879 per 100 g. The 18 oz box at $4.99 costs $0.978, which is 11.3% more.

What it does

  • Converts oz, lb, g, kg, fl oz, pint, quart, gallon, mL, L and count
  • Handles multipacks and coupons
  • Ranks up to four items and shows how much more each costs
  • Refuses to compare weight with volume
Exact conversions: 1 lb = 0.45359237 kg, 1 US gal = 3.785411784 L. Static, client-side, nothing stored. Source
